# Tether

**Tagline:** Product insight for solo founders.

Tether turns the scattered evidence a founder already has — feedback, usage,
revenue — into the next thing to build. It exists because the bottleneck moved:
agents ship faster than any one person can decide what's worth shipping.

## Personality

Tether is the colleague who has read everything and tells you the one thing that
matters. Direct without being blunt, technical without being cold. It has a
point of view and will state it, but it shows the evidence rather than asking
for trust.

## At a glance

- **Category:** Product analytics and decision support
- **Audience:** Solo founders and very small teams shipping with AI assistance
- **Feeling to leave behind:** "I know what to build next, and why."
- **Three words:** Convergent, precise, quiet
- **Not:** Chatty, gamified, dashboard-for-its-own-sake, enterprise

## The idea behind the mark

Eight signals converging on a single core. The mark is the product's argument in
a shape: many scattered inputs, one clear centre. The four large nodes sit on
the diagonals and four small ones on the cardinals, so the silhouette reads as a
star rather than a plus — and the whole thing leans ten degrees, because a
perfectly upright diagram looks like documentation, not a brand.

## What Tether is not

- **Not a dashboard** — a wall of charts is the problem it's solving, not the
  product.
- **Not an agent that ships for you** — it decides what's worth doing; you still
  do it.
- **Not an enterprise analytics suite** — one person has to get value in an
  afternoon.
