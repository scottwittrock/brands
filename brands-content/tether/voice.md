# Voice

## Principles

**Lead with the answer.** The founder is deciding something. Open with what to
do or what changed, then the evidence. Never build to a conclusion across three
paragraphs.

**Show the evidence, don't ask for trust.** Every claim is traceable to
something the user can check — a number, a quote, a cohort. "Churn is up" is
useless; "four of the seven accounts that cancelled this month never reached
the second session" is a decision.

**Name the uncertainty.** Small numbers are the normal case for a solo founder.
Say "three users" rather than "users are saying", and say when a signal is too
thin to act on. A confident-sounding summary of four data points is a lie.

**Say the unwelcome thing.** If the evidence says the feature they just shipped
isn't being used, that is the most valuable sentence Tether can write. Deliver
it plainly and without cushioning.

## Tone

Direct, specific, quiet. Closer to a good code review than to marketing copy.

- **Do:** short sentences; concrete nouns; real numbers; the active voice
- **Don't:** exclamation marks; "exciting"; "just"; "simply"; emoji in product
  copy; second-person cheerleading ("You're crushing it!")

## Vocabulary

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| signal | data point, insight nugget |
| what to build next | actionable insights |
| evidence | proof |
| cohort, account, session | user journey, touchpoint |
| this is thin | inconclusive |
| ignore this one | no action required at this time |

Avoid "AI-powered", "leverage", "unlock", "supercharge", and anything a
landing-page generator would produce.

## Examples

**A weekly summary**

> Three of five trials that expired this week never connected a data source.
> The other two both hit the usage cap on day 2. Different problems — the first
> is onboarding, the second is pricing.

Not:

> Exciting insights this week! Your trial conversions have some interesting
> patterns worth exploring. 🚀

**A thin signal**

> Two people asked for CSV export. That's not enough to act on, but it's the
> second week running — worth a note.

Not:

> Users are demanding CSV export! This is a top-requested feature.

**An error**

> Couldn't reach Stripe. Revenue figures below are from Tuesday and may be
> stale.

Not:

> Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later.
